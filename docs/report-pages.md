# Report Tour — Purchase & Supply

2 pages tracking incoming goods receipts from suppliers — value, quantity, and
transaction volume, benchmarked against prior year.

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Supply** | Monthly purchase value and quantity trend vs. prior year, weekly receiving-transaction volume, and a transaction-level detail table (date, invoice number, product, brand, division, quantity, value, product count) filterable by receipt/invoice ID. |
| **Purchases** | Brand and product-level purchase value/quantity with YoY growth index, a purchase-share-by-brand donut, and a brand-level current-vs-prior-year comparison chart. |

Both pages share the filter panel (date range, division/brand, product) and
navigator shell used across the solution.
